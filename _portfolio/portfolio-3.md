### 1. Project Overview

This application is designed to automate inventory management, sales, and revenue tracking processes for small and medium-sized pharmacies.

### 2. Technologies Used

- **Main Language:** Java.
- **Interface:** Java Swing.
- **Database:** SQL Server.

### 3. Main Features

- **Inventory Management:** Track inventory levels by batch.
- **Sales Management:** Quickly generate invoices, calculate total amount and VAT, and deduct inventory levels immediately upon invoice issuance.
- **Category Management:** Classify medications by group (antibiotics, painkillers, dietary supplements, etc.), manage supplier information and units of measurement (tablets, blisters, boxes).
- **Statistics & Reports:** Generate daily/monthly/yearly revenue reports.

### 4. Achieved Results

- The system has been completed.
- The UI/UX interface is intuitive and smooth on the desktop.
